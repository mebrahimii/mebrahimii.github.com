About Me

Mahdi Ebrahimi is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at California State University, Northridge (CSUN). His research focuses on Big Data, data-intensive systems, cloud computing, and optimization.

His current work explores the intersection of Big Data and Artificial Intelligence, particularly scalable AI-powered data systems and intelligent data analytics. His research has appeared in peer-reviewed journals and conference proceedings, including IEEE Big Data venues.

Research Areas

My research centers on large-scale data systems and their intersection with cloud computing, optimization, and modern AI.

▦

Big Data & Distributed Systems

Large-scale data processing, Apache Spark, distributed analytics, scientific workflows, and scalable data management.

✦

AI-Powered Data Systems

Scalable AI systems that integrate LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation, vector retrieval, and large-scale data processing.

☁

Cloud Computing & Optimization

Resource allocation, data placement, workflow scheduling, and optimization of data-intensive cloud applications.

▥

Intelligent Data Centers

AI- and data-driven approaches to workload scheduling, energy efficiency, thermal management, and resource optimization.
